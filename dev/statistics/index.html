Statistics beyond the t-ratio

Besides the t-ratio the package has the other statistics of the limit experiment of the Dickey-Fuller regression, the pair (U, V) of the numerator and the denominator: the classical engine for the classical cases, the general engine for any design (see Two engines). Every function below takes a case code or a design in place of cas, and its docstring (?coef_cdf) has the arguments; how the half-plane statistics and the moments are computed is in The devices of Bao and Kan.

The classical functions

functioncomputes
coef_cdf(y, cas, c = 0; c0, ...), coef_icdf(p, cas, c = 0; tol, ...)the coefficient statistic n(phi^ - phi0), whose limit is (U - c0 V)/V
coef_pdf(y, cas, c = 0; c0, g0), coef_mom(p, cas, c = 0; c0, g0)its density and moments
adfmax_cdf(x, cas, c = 0), adfmax_pdf, adfmax_icdf(p, cas, c = 0; tol, x0)the ADFmax statistic of Leybourne (1995), the larger of the t-ratios of the forward and the reverse regression (cases DU, DT, SU, ST)
ltratio_region(bnd, cas = "DK", c = 0)P{U <= g(V)} for a line, the parabola of the t-ratio, the boundary of the weighted-average-power test of the companion paper or a function g
ltratio_lincdf(b, a, cas, c = 0; cbar, N, umax)the distribution of the linear form U - aV (a = NaN: of V), case DK or DG; the point-optimal statistics are of this form
power_envelope(c, cas = "DK"; alpha)the asymptotic power envelope of Elliott, Rothenberg and Stock (1996), case DK (= DU) or DG

Every test of the unit root in the limit experiment of case DK rejects on a region of the plane of (U, V), and the first part of the conditional integral representation of the companion paper, the probability of a region of the plane, gives its probability for every boundary g by one outer integral over V; the ADFmax statistic rejects on the region between two curves of the plane. The boundary bnd of ltratio_region is a NamedTuple (or a Dict): (type = "line", a, b), (type = "parabola", x, c0), (type = "wap", k, omega) or (type = "fun", g) with g a function of a scalar, optionally with v0 and brks.

julia> coef_cdf(-14.0936, "DU", -10)                  # power of the 5% coefficient test at c = -10
0.45925256320763047

julia> coef_mom(1, "DK")                              # the mean of n(phi^ - 1) at the unit root
1-element Vector{Float64}:
 -1.781430171277838

julia> adfmax_icdf(0.05, "DU")                        # 5% critical value of ADFmax with a constant
-2.435416499432509

julia> ltratio_region((type = "wap", k = 3.6658, omega = 10), "DK")   # size of the WAP test of the companion paper
0.049999622834651807

julia> ltratio_lincdf(-0.2639, -3.5, "DK", -7)        # power of the point-optimal test against cbar = -7 at c = -7
0.49938157210469375

julia> power_envelope(-7)                             # the power envelope at c = -7
0.4994019828155684

The general engine

The general engine's own functions are exported as well: dist_cdf(D, xs, c; c0, start, gamma0, gls, method), dist_pdf, dist_quantile, dist_moments (the t-ratio), coef_pdf, coef_quantile, coef_cdf_gp (the coefficient statistic), lin_cdf, lin_pdf, lin_quantile, v_cdf, v_pdf (the linear form U - aV and the law of V), adfmax_quantile, region_prob, local_power, simulate_tratio, simulate_gls, simulate_first and the designs; its coef_cdf, adfmax_cdf, adfmax_pdf and power_envelope, whose names are the classical functions', are DFLimit.DFDet.coef_cdf and so on. Note the order of the arguments: the general engine's own functions take the design first (dist_cdf(D, xs, c)), the classical functions the points first (ltratio_cdf(x, D, c)).

The statistics of the lead paper beyond the t-ratio

The statistics of the lead paper beyond the t-ratio are functions of the general engine: the point-optimal statistic of Elliott, Rothenberg and Stock and the power envelope for any design (pt_cdf(D, qs, cbar, c), pt_pdf, pt_quantile(D, p, cbar), pt_power(D, cbar, c), pt_envelope(D, cs), also through power_envelope(c, D); for the intercept and the linear trend it is the envelope of the classical construction to 1e-8), the LM statistic of Schmidt and Phillips and of Amsler and Lee (lm_cdf(D, xs, c), lm_quantile(D, p), lm_power(D, cs); 5% value -3.0230 for the linear trend; for trend functions whose span contains the constant and a linear trend, an ArgumentError otherwise, DFDet.span_has_constant) and the companions of the GLS-detrended t-ratio, MZalpha (`mzacdf(D, ys, c; gls),mzaquantile) and MSB (msbcdf(D, xs, c; gls),msb_quantile`).

julia> D = perron(:C, 0.5);                           # a break in level and slope at the middle of the sample

julia> local_power(D, [-5.0, -10.0, -20.0])           # the power of the 5% test
3-element Vector{Float64}:
 0.06689363562246417
 0.11488561464598873
 0.34595668565582766

julia> pt_envelope(D, [-5.0, -10.0, -20.0])           # the power envelope of the design
3-element Vector{Float64}:
 0.08062917914287143
 0.17888853618084333
 0.606153452406375

julia> lm_quantile(perron(:B, 0.5), 0.05)             # the LM test with a slope break
-3.6474783394296186
