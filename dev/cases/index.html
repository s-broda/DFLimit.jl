Case codes and arguments

The case cas is a two-letter code: D (fixed start, g0, default 0) or S (stationary start, c <= -1e-4), followed by K (no deterministic terms, tau), U (intercept, taumu) or T (intercept and trend, tautau); "DG" is the GLS-detrended trend case (DF-GLS, cbar = -13.5); in place of the case a design of the general engine may be given (see Two engines). method is :auto (at each point the fastest representation that one evaluation gives to well below 1e-5), :single or :line (the line integral representation of the companion paper), :double or :conditional (the conditional integral representation, also of the companion paper) or :contour (the contour integral representation: the inversion formula on paths of the lead paper, around the singularities of the moment generating function, with the integral over v in closed form and the paths in s and t deformed, a double integral with an explicit integrand; a fixed start in cases DU and DT only, and there the choice of :auto). By default (verified = false) every value is one evaluation by one method, with a warning, once per call, where the parameters lie in a regime in which one evaluation is known to be brittle; verified = true gives the checked computation (error estimates and fallbacks, at 1 to 4.7 times the cost of the default, 2.9 to 4.7 with a fixed start, up to 24 times where the default is the general engine's paths, case SU at c = c0 = -10, and more in the tails below 1e-3, which it recomputes; November 26, 2026). x, y and the probabilities p may be arrays. The boundary bnd of ltratio_region is a NamedTuple (or a Dict): (type = "line", a, b), (type = "parabola", x, c0), (type = "wap", k, omega) or (type = "fun", g) with g a function of a scalar, optionally with v0 and brks.

The functions return the values only (the MATLAB functions also return an info structure); every one has a docstring (?ltratio_cdf). Below c = -550, and with a fixed start g0 outside the region in which the programs are checked, they raise an ArgumentError that says so. The engine's functions that are not exported are reached through the module DFLimit.DFExact, among them the representations, which take the model number 1, 2, 3, 4 for K, U, T, G: the line integral DFLimit.DFExact.cdf(q, model; c, c0, g0, start = :zero) (start = :stationary for a stationary start), DFLimit.DFExact.density and DFLimit.DFExact.moments(model; c, c0, g0, start), the conditional integral DFLimit.DFExact.tr_limdist(x, model, c; c0, g0, start, pdf) and the contour integral DFLimit.DFExact.tr_contour(x, model, c; c0, g0, pdf) (the comments of src/DFExact.jl describe them, and dev/README.md the range of c in which the rules are set).
