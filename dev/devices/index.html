The devices of Bao and Kan

The half-plane statistics of the six classical cases, the coefficient statistic (coef_cdf, coef_pdf, coef_icdf, coef_mom), the linear form U - aV (ltratio_lincdf) and the power envelope (power_envelope), and the moments of the t-ratio (ltratio_mom) are computed by default with the numerical devices of Yong Bao and Raymond Kan, "Exact Finite-Sample Distribution and Moments of the Least Squares Estimator in First-Order Autoregressions: O(1) Algorithms", ported to the limits of this package in the module DFLimit.DFKan (src/DFKan*.jl). The joint characteristic function of the limit pair comes in closed forms that are free of cancellation on both sides of the unit root, with the branch of the square root fixed at each point from factors whose arguments are known, and in Taylor-mode arithmetic (jets), so that its derivatives come out of the same assembly. A statistic that rejects on a half-plane is then one inversion along a ray, by adaptive quadrature over decades with the tail beyond S = 400/|w| (w the slope of the dominant phase) completed by repeated integration by parts, the derivatives of the slowly varying factor read off a jet of order eight. The moments of the t-ratio are one-dimensional integrals over s of the j-th derivative of the moment generating function along its affine path, which one jet of order j gives at each node of an adaptive quadrature in log s (method = :jets; before, Cauchy integrals on a circle); the quantile searches of the t-ratio (ltratio_icdf, also where the rule gives them to the general engine) start from the normal approximation with these moments (DFLimit.mom_start; before, the engines' circles; DFLimit.MOM_START[] = false restores that). Every value comes with an error estimate: err = true returns (values, errors), tol (1e-12) is the absolute tolerance, and a warning names the values whose estimate exceeds it. method = :engine is the computation of before (the engines, by the rule of autoengine; the t-ratio at x = 0 for the coefficient statistic), which reproduces those values, and engine = :classical or :general, the engines' own keywords and case DG take it as well; method = :inversion forces the single inversion; verified = true recomputes by the engines' checked computation the values whose estimate exceeds the tolerance.

The general engine has the same inversion for the statistics of a design whose transform it evaluates point by point: the point-optimal statistic (pt_cdf, pt_pdf, pt_quantile, pt_power, pt_envelope, and through them power_envelope for designs beyond the classical cases) and coef_cdf_gp (the coefficient statistic by Gil-Pelaez, the independent check of the contour integral), with the branch of the square root of the determinant fixed at each point from the pivots of the precision matrix, Bao and Kan's lemma on accretive matrices (DFDet.DetCF, src/DFKan_det.jl), in place of its continuation along the fixed grid of 60,000 points. method = :grid (or the keywords N, umax) gives the grid of before with its refusals. The point-optimal statistic agrees with the contour integral of the general engine on its quadratic form (the paths around the roots, an independent evaluation) to 3.3e-12 from c = -30 to 4 (an intercept at cbar = -7, a linear trend at -13.5, Perron's model B at -17.8, a quadratic trend at -20; the 1 to 99% points), where the grid is off by up to 3.8e-10 at c = 3 and refuses at c = 4 and 5; the single inversion takes 1.5 to 11 ms a value against 46 to 460 ms for the grid. Above c = 4 it can take seconds a value (Perron's model B: 0.01 to 0.04 s at c = 5, 1.1 to 2.2 s at c = 6), and the grid with its refusal stays the default there; the single inversion has no limit on q (the grid refused |q| > 1000). The same devices fix the branches inside the engines: the classical engine's P(t), M0(t) and the labels of the roots of its line and contour integrals (`DFExact.tpartspoint`) and the argument of log P(t) in the provider of the general engine's line integral are determined at each point instead of being continued along the grids, to rounding (5.7e-14) the values of before, at the same cost.

At a = 0 (the law of U alone, whose transform decays like a power, with the edge of its support where the tail start runs away) the linear form stays with the engines' closed form; at c = 7 in case DK the classical code differs from the single inversion by 2e-8 at the 99.9% point with a = 2, which no third computation settled, so the default there is unchanged. Times are warm minima (7 timed calls after a warm-up, 3 from 1 s on) on four threads of an M4 laptop on mains power with nothing else running (November 26, 2026), against method = :engine or the grid in the same session.
