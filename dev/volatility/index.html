Volatility and persistence regimes

The Dickey-Fuller t-ratio when the standard deviation of the innovations changes at known dates, and, if wanted, the local-to-unity parameter with it. The data are yt = d(t/n)'beta + ut with ut = phit u{t-1} + omega(t/n) et, u0 = 0, et independent with unit variance, phi_t = 1 + c(t/n)/n, and omega and c constant on the regimes between the break dates (fractions of the sample); the statistic is the t-ratio of the lagged level in the least squares regression on d(t/n), with the usual variance estimate. Its limit is (U - c0 V)/sqrt(V), U and V the functionals of the general engine of the process dJ = c(r) J dr + omega(r) dW, J(0) = 0, with omega scaled so that the integral of omega^2 is one (the variance estimate converges to the average of omega^2, and the statistic does not depend on the scale of omega).

The variants of the engine carry over to the regimes: GLS detrending (gls = cbar), the statistic that detrends first (detrend_first), and a stationary initial observation (start = :stationary). Neither the detrending nor the variance estimate uses the variance path; the regimes change the law of the path alone. For a fixed initial observation the transform is computed and the distribution is not (below).

The transform

On the pieces of the design refined at the regime boundaries let thetaj = sqrt(cj^2 - 2t omegaj^2) and gj = (cj + thetaj)/(2 omegaj^2). Girsanov's theorem, piece by piece, turns the tilt by exp(t int J^2) into the process dJ = -theta(r) J dr + omega(r) dW, further tilted by exp{glast J(1)^2 + sumj (gj - g{j+1}) J(bj)^2} at the right ends b_j of the pieces, and E exp(sU + tV) = e^{-s/2} D(s,t)^{-1/2} with

D(s,t) = exp{sum_j (c_j + theta_j) L_j} det{I - 2 (s A + t B + Gamma) Sigma},

A and B the design's matrices padded with zeros for J at the regime boundaries, Gamma the diagonal matrix of the tilts and Sigma the covariance of the functionals under the tilted process (the engine's transfer functions with the exponent and the variance of each piece). This is the engine's transform with a diagonal tilt in place of gamma E11, and everything downstream of the determinant is the engine's code: the outer contour (the hyperbola, steepened while a group of roots leaves its side of the imaginary axis), the continuation of the roots and of log P along it, the inner integrals around the roots, the rules of one evaluation, the checked computation and the quantile search. (The steepening while either group leaves its side, where the engine looked at the positive group only, changes no path of the engine's own designs: 464 configurations with zero, fixed and stationary starts and GLS detrending, c from -480 to 14.) The calls with a volatility path take the general engine whatever the rule of the engines (autoengine), and engine = :classical refuses them. Without deterministic terms D is the first entry of a product of two-by-two transfer matrices of the regimes, which the tests use as an independent check.

Detrending and the start

Each variant enters through the one part of the transform that it changes.

GLS detrending and the statistic that detrends first change the matrices A and B alone. The design is refined at the regime boundaries and prepared as the engine prepares it (prep_gls at cbar, or the numerator of detrend_first): the functionals are the path at one and at the dates of the prepared design and its integrals against the basis of that design, and the law of the path enters as above, through the exponent, Gamma and Sigma. With GLS detrending the numerator has rank one, one positive root, as at a constant variance.

A normal start, J(0) centred normal with variance v0 and independent of W, makes J(0) one more functional. With h the mean of the functionals under the tilted process started at one, the covariance of (ell, J(0)) is [Sigma + v0 h h', v0 h; v0 h', v0], the matrices are those of the engine's stationary start (A bordered by b/2 and -1/2, B by zeros), and Gamma gains -g1 at the start. start = :stationary is the stationary start of the first regime, v0 = omega1^2/(-2 c_1), which needs c < 0 there.

A fixed start J(0) = gamma0 shifts the mean of the functionals to gamma0 h, and log M = log M0 + gamma0^2 Q(s,t) with M0 the transform of the zero start and Q the exponent of the engine's fixed start at the tilt t B + Gamma, with g1 in place of gamma. J(0) is in the units of the path, whose innovation variance averages one over the sample: y0 = gamma0 sigma sqrt(n) with sigma^2 the average innovation variance. A normal start is the average of the fixed one over gamma0, DN = D0 (1 - 2 v0 Q), which the tests use in both directions. The exponent is DFLimit.DFDet.vol_qf. The distribution with a fixed start is not computed under regimes, and dist_cdf, dist_pdf, dist_quantile and the classical functions raise an ArgumentError for start = :fixed with a nonzero gamma0 and more than one regime: the engine lays the paths of a fixed start by rules made for a constant variance and persistence, and under regimes they lose accuracy or fail for starts of moderate size (the start counts in the units of the first regime's standard deviation; with an intercept and a persistence that changes, or far from the unit root, no angle of the outer contour gave a value in November 2026).

A start is combined with least squares detrending in the one-step regression, as in the engine: with GLS detrending or with the statistic that detrends first the limit is derived for a zero start, and a start raises an ArgumentError.

Calls

callcomputes
volpath(breaks, omega)the regimes: break dates in (0, 1), the standard deviations (any positive scale)
dist_cdf(D, xs, c; vol), dist_pdf, dist_quantile(D, p, c; vol)the t-ratio of any design D under the regimes
the same with gls = cbarGLS detrending at 1 + cbar/n
the same with detrend_first(D)the statistic that detrends first
the same with start = :stationarythe stationary start of the first regime, J(0) ~ N(0, omega1^2/(-2 c1))
ltratio_cdf(x, cas_or_D, c; vol), ltratio_pdf, ltratio_icdfthe same through the classical functions (a case string or a design; "SU", "ST" the stationary start, "DG" GLS detrending)
hegy_t3_cdf(D, xs, c; vol), hegy_t3_pdf, hegy_t3_quantilethe seasonal t-ratio of Dickey-Fuller type under the regimes (Seasonal unit roots)

The local-to-unity parameter c is a number, or a vector with one value per regime (changing persistence); c0, the local null value of the t-ratio, is a number as always. A volatility path with one regime and a scalar c is the engine itself. The extension covers least squares detrending with a zero or a stationary start, and with a zero start GLS detrending and the statistic that detrends first, every c at most 0 (above the unit root it is not implemented) and at least -480, and the inversion on paths (method = :auto or :paths; the line integral is not derived for this transform). verified = true is the engine's checked computation, with the variant contours built for the regimes.

The parts of the transform are functions of the general engine, DFLimit.DFDet: vol_model(D, V, c; start, g0, gls) builds the model of a design under the regimes (and takes v0, the variance of a normal start other than the stationary one), vol_dval(VM, s, t) is the determinant, vol_qf(VM, s, t) the exponent of the fixed start, vol_roots(VM, t) the roots in s at a real t, tstar(VM, 0) the first zero of D(0, .), and vol_path(VM) the outer contour with the roots continued along it.

Examples

julia> V = volpath([0.1], [1.0, 3.0])               # the standard deviation triples after a tenth of the sample
volpath([0.1], [0.349215, 1.04765])

julia> cv = ltratio_icdf(0.05, "DU")                # the 5% critical value at a constant variance
-2.8615688636519616

julia> ltratio_cdf(cv, "DU"; vol = V)               # the size of that test under the variance break
0.02278491904449314

julia> ltratio_icdf(0.05, "DU"; vol = V)            # the 5% critical value under the break
-2.579981541980405

julia> ltratio_cdf(cv, "DU", [0.0, -20.0]; vol = volpath([0.5]))   # power when c falls from 0 to -20 at mid-sample
0.13186507163798253

julia> dist_quantile(perron(:C, 0.4), [0.01, 0.05], -5.0; vol = volpath([0.6], [3.0, 1.0]))
2-element Vector{Float64}:
 -5.272473428374128
 -4.674747457616461

The last line is a break in level and slope at 0.4 with the standard deviation falling to a third at 0.6, under the local alternative c = -5.

Accuracy and cost

At a constant variance with a dummy boundary the distribution function and the density are those of the engine's paths to 1e-15, at c = 0, -10 and -100; the transform equals the engine's determinant to 1e-10 relative and, without deterministic terms, the product of the transfer matrices to 1e-11; extrapolated in 1/n, the exact moment generating function of the regression in samples of 200, 400 and 800 agrees with it to 2e-5. The values of the prototype (the table of sizes at eight decimals, its quantiles at four, the power under changing persistence), which were checked there against simulations of the regression and of the limit functional, are reproduced to their printed digits. The rule of one evaluation is the engine's (path_region); on the 36 cells of the table it is within 4e-12 of the present rule.

GLS detrending, the statistic that detrends first and the determinant of a normal start were computed for the lead paper by a prototype among its replication code before the package had them; its values are kept in test/ref/regime_ref.txt. On the prototype's contour and with its nodes (rule = :present) the package reproduces the distribution function and the density to 2e-15 (291 evaluations, among them the 216 cells of the paper's table of sizes and critical values under one variance break and the p-values of its illustration), the determinant, the roots and t* to 1e-13, and the 369 quantiles to 2e-10, the tolerance of the searches. The default rule of one evaluation is within 2.2e-10 of the prototype in all of these cases but one (the quadratic trend detrended first under three regimes with their own c: 6.5e-9). The prototype's determinant of a normal start and its determinant of the zero start yield the exponent of the fixed start, which `volqf` reproduces to 1.4e-10.

The stationary start, which the prototype did not invert, agrees with the engine's at a constant variance with a dummy boundary to 1e-14; its transform agrees with the exact moment generating function of the regression in samples of 200, 400 and 800, extrapolated in 1/n, to 2e-5; and its distribution function equals the average over a normal start of the distribution function with a fixed start, computed from the zero start's roots and the exponent of the start, to 5e-11.

On a wider grid (140 cases: five designs with a stationary start, five with GLS detrending and four detrended first, under ten paths with ratios of the standard deviations up to 9 and c down to -150, November 27, 2026) the default rule is within 1e-6 of the checked computation in the distribution function, with two exceptions, both announced. Where the computed value in the upper tail exceeds one by more than 1e-8 the call fails with the engine's range error (one case, a stationary start with a break in level and slope; rule = :present or :fine computes it). And with GLS detrending at a cbar near zero (-2), or beside a standard deviation that rises ninefold late in the sample, no hyperbola keeps the positive root right of the origin and the outer contour is the vertical line: the engine warns there, and the values differed by up to 5e-4 between rules or the call failed.

Times on warm calls (four threads of an M4 laptop on mains power with nothing else running, November 26, 2026): the outer contour with its roots 3 to 19 ms, one value of F and f 2 to 5 ms, a 5% quantile 9 to 45 ms (no deterministic terms, an intercept, a linear trend with the variance tripling at mid-sample; a break in level and slope at 0.4 with the variance falling at 0.6), 1.4 to 3.6 times faster than the prototype in the same session: the thin rule of one evaluation, the engine's static arrays for the small matrices of the transform, its continuation of the roots between the nodes only where a step is not safe, and t_* from the Nystrom estimate rather than from two discretized regressions.
