The rules of one evaluation

One evaluation of the general engine's inversion on paths (dist_cdf, dist_pdf, dist_quantile and, through them, the classical functions where the rule autoengine takes the general engine) takes a thinner rule than before November 2026 where it holds (DFDet.PATH_RULES, DFDet.path_region; keyword rule, :auto by default): the outer hyperbola with 1.5 times the step and the U-shapes with 16 nodes per panel and 32 on the tails of their rays for 24 and 40 (32 and 48 for 48 and 64 with a fixed start), 0.49 times the time of one evaluation (the median over 404 configurations, 0.44 to 0.69, warm calls on mains power, November 26, 2026; the thin inner rule on the present hyperbola 0.72). The regions, a rule in (the start, whether the design has deterministic terms, c, c0) that the C engine and the MATLAB programs can take over:

On the vertical line (above the unit root, where no hyperbola keeps the positive roots right of the origin) the line keeps its panels and only the inner rule thins. rule = :present gives the rule of before, verified = true takes it (and its variants), and hfac, n, ntail override the rule. A sweep of 822 configurations (no deterministic terms, an intercept, linear and quadratic trends, Perron's model B with one break and model C with two, the linear trend GLS-detrended at -13.5 and detrending first; zero, fixed (g0 = 1) and stationary starts; c from 2 to -60 and c0 in {0, 2, -2, 5, -5, c}; F and f at the 1e-5 to 1 - 1e-5 points; scratch/rules/sweep1.jl of the branch kan-integration) set the regions, and a dense one checked them (scratch/rules/dense3.jl: 736 configurations, x from the mean - 6 sd to the mean + 6 sd in steps of 0.5 sd, c from 2 to -60 and at 5 and -480; the rule within 7.6e-10 of the present rule in F and 4e-9 of the peak in f wherever it is not the present one or a finer one). The criterion: within max(1e-9, twice the present rule's own error) in F and max(1e-8 of the largest density, twice its own) in f, against a reference with half the outer step and twice the inner nodes. A first thin rule with 12 and 24 nodes held at the sweep's points but lost up to 9e-8 of the peak of the density between them near the unit root (case DK at c = 0.5 near the mode), which 16 and 32 nodes do not. Far from the unit root the reference is not the more accurate: in case DK at c = -60, c0 = 2 the present rule is within 1.7e-10 of the classical engine's checked computation, the reference 6.7e-10. On the line with c0 > 0 at x < 0 (two roots of the parabola) the line keeps its Gauss-Legendre rule and its warning, and the present rule is off by up to 2.7e-5 there (case DK at c = 5, c0 = 2), as is the thin one; method = :auto takes the line integral at these points where it covers the design (DFDet.line_vline). The extensions take the same rules: the volatility transform that of path_region for the zero-start model of its design at the smallest c of its regimes, the normal average of rho that of each of its calls of the distribution function.

The classical engine's contour integral (a fixed start in cases DU, DT; DFExact.ctr_rule, keyword rule of tr_contour) takes, for one evaluation, 32 nodes per panel and ray of the U-shapes for 64 and the outer step 1.5 times as long where they hold: 0.39 times the time near the unit root (median; 0.38 to 0.41; warm calls on mains power, November 26, 2026), the 32 nodes on the present step above the unit root (c > 1, 0.57 times), 64 nodes on the longer step with c0 > 0 below c = -2.5 and everywhere below c = -10 (0.69 times), and the present rule where |c g0| > 20 (there the longer step lost up to 2.4e-2 in F, case DT at c = -30, g0 = 3). Against the checked computation on a dense grid of x (129 configurations: cases DU with g0 0.5 and 1.5, DT with 1 and 3, c from 2 to -60, c0 in {0, 2, -2, -5, c}) every configuration holds the criterion, within 6.2e-11 in F. Far from the unit root the present rule's own one evaluation was off: with c0 > 0 in the density by up to 5e-6 of the peak (case DU at c = -30, g0 = 0.5, c0 = 2), 4.2e-3 (c = -75, c0 = 5) and 2.2e-2 (c = -60, g0 = 1.5, c0 = 5), and where |c g0| > 60 with any c0 in the distribution function by up to 3.9e-5 (case DU, c = -60, g0 = 1.5, c0 = -2) and 6.3e-6 (c = -100, g0 = 0.9); its checked computation was off in the density by up to 8.4e-6 of the peak. The rule of the engines (autoengine) gives these cells to the general engine: with c0 > 0 below c = -20 (below c = -10 where |c g0| > 20), and with any c0 below c = -20 where |c g0| > 60. There the general engine's density is the derivative of the distribution function on which the two engines agree (to 1e-10 of the peak, by Richardson's central difference; scratch/rules/probe_ctr_far5.jl), and it takes 0.35 to 0.86 times the time of the classical one evaluation down to c = -45, up to 1.9 times at c = -60 and 1.1 to 6 times at c = -75 and -100 (November 26, 2026, on mains power).

The classical engine's conditional integral (one evaluation, DFExact.cond_rule) takes 48 nodes on the path of each inner inversion and of the integral over l for 96 with a stationary start for c <= -2, with a zero start in cases DU, DT for c >= -5 and in case DK for c <= -13.5 (medians of warm calls, F and f at 13 points, November 26, 2026, on mains power: 0.60 times the time with a stationary start, 0.78 and 0.84 in cases DU, DT with a zero start, 0.76 in case DK, where the conditional integral takes 22 to 64 ms), and 64 with a stationary start for c > -2; a fixed start (measured at one configuration only) and c > 2 keep 96. Against the rule with 192 nodes and the outer tolerance 1e-14 on a dense grid of x (150 configurations: cases DK, DU, DT with a zero start, c from 2 to -100, and SK, SU, ST, c from -0.5 to -50, c0 in {2, -2, -5, c}) the regions hold the criterion. Elsewhere 48 nodes lost up to 1e-3 far from the unit root with a zero start in cases DU, DT (1.8e-8 of the peak of the density at c = -13.5), 7e-8 of the peak in case SK at c = -0.5, and 7e-7 with a fixed start (case DU, c = 2, c0 = -2, g0 = 0.5). The outer tolerance 1e-10 in place of 1e-12 held as well but gained nothing measurable (0.85 against 0.90 with a zero start) and is not taken.
