Which engine

dev/engine_table.jl (dev/engine_table.txt) times both engines in each cell (4 threads, the warm time of one call, the minimum of 7 timed calls after a warm-up, of 3 from 1 s on; F and f at x = mean + (-2, -1, 0, 1, 2) sd for the t-ratio) and compares their values; where they differ by more than 1e-8 it compares each with the other's checked computation (verified = true). The rule, autoengine in src/merged.jl, takes the faster engine at the same accuracy, and the right one where they disagree. It was re-derived on November 26, 2026, after the thinner rules of one evaluation had halved the time of the general engine's paths: from interleaved timings of the two engines in each cell (the two choices alternated in one process, warm, the minimum of 12 rounds each; two runs, a cell moved only where the other choice was at least 1.3 times faster in both) and from checks of the new choice's one evaluation against the other engine's checked computation on 41 points from the 0.01% to the 99.99% point over the cell's region (within 1e-7 in F, in f of the peak and in the quantiles' probabilities). The times in the table below are those of dev/engine_table.txt (the run of November 26, 2026 on mains power with nothing else running, an M4 laptop, four threads, about ten minutes for the whole table; the ranges over the table's cells of each row).

Within the general engine, dist_cdf, dist_pdf and dist_quantile (and the classical functions with a design, through them) choose between two representations by the keyword method: :auto (the default, DFDet.line_auto) takes the line integral with the explicit kernel where it covers the design and c0 = 0, except above the unit root for 0 < c <= 1.5 with one regressor (the intercept) and 0 < c <= 2 with two or more, and with c0 > 0 at the points x < 0 where the outer contour of the paths is the vertical line (DFDet.line_vline; the quantiles keep the paths there); the inversion on paths around the roots otherwise; :line forces the line integral (an ArgumentError with the reason where it does not cover the design, the start or the options) and :paths the inversion on paths. The line integral covers the designs whose numerator has rank one or two: no deterministic terms, the continuous designs that do not vanish at the origin and whose span is closed under differentiation (polynomial trends of any order, an intercept with sines and cosines), and detrend_first of a continuous design that does not vanish at the origin, with a zero or a stationary start and least squares detrending (not GLS detrending, a fixed start or a design with a jump). It is the classical engine's integrator with P(t) and the two roots supplied by the general engine's transform (src/DFDet_line.jl). On warm calls at c0 = 0 (November 26, 2026, on mains power; F and f at five points and the 5% quantile, four threads; c = 0, -10, -50) it is up to 3.9 times faster than the paths at and below the unit root (median 1.4), except far from it with two or more regressors, where the paths are as fast or faster (at c = -50 1.05 to 1.25 times for F and f with the quadratic trend, the intercept with a sine and a cosine and the trend with a stationary start, and 1.04 to 1.6 times for the 5% quantile in most designs). Above the unit root the paths are 1.3 to 3.7 times faster than the line integral (and 1.8 to 2.4 times faster than the classical engine for the intercept and the trend) while their outer hyperbola keeps its angle of 45 degrees: for the intercept up to c = 1.5, for the trends, the trend detrended first and the intercept with a sine and a cosine up to c = 2 or 2.5, for the quadratic trend up to c = 3; beyond, as the hyperbola steepens, the line integral is the faster again (1.3 times for the intercept at c = 2, 1.2 to 9 times for every design at c = 3 except the quadratic trend), and without deterministic terms it stays the faster from c = 1 on. With c0 != 0 the line integral is 4.4 to 11 times slower, hence the paths there, except with c0 > 0 on the vertical line: there the parabola X(v) of a point x < 0 has two positive roots, whose parts the line's rule does not resolve, and the paths' one evaluation was off by up to 1e-3 in the lower tail (an intercept with a sine and a cosine at c = 5, c0 = 2), where the line integral is accurate. A case string or a classical design with engine = :general therefore takes the general engine's line integral at c0 = 0 below the unit root (as the general column of the table below, except case DG, which the line integral does not cover) and its paths above it as described; with method = :paths it takes the paths.

Where the classical engine computes, its values are those of engine = :classical bit for bit; where the rule takes the general engine for a case string, the values move within the differences above (in the cells moved on November 26, 2026 within 1e-7). The borders were swept: the stationary start at c = -3e-3 (cases SU, ST: the general engine within 4e-11 of the classical at c = -3e-3, off by 8e-10 at c = -1e-3), the moments next to zero (case SK down to c = -3e-3, cases SU, ST down to c = -1), the power envelope (case DK within 3e-12 of the classical down to c = -0.01) and the tangency of case DK (the window 1e-3 above x*). The tests follow the rule (test/runtests.jl, test/engine): one stored value changed, the power envelope of case DK at c = -7, now 0.49940198281562 (it was 0.49940198260314117, the classical engine's bisection of the critical value to 1e-9; the same construction with the critical value bisected to 1e-14 is within 8e-13 of the new value), and the checks of the classical engine's own computations (ADFmax with a stationary start, the moments, quantiles and default values of its test grid far from the unit root, the power envelope) force engine = :classical, so that they keep their stored values. Three defects of the general engine are repaired in src/DFDet.jl (see NEWS): the law of V on the vertical line (off by 2e-3 at c = 5, and with GLS detrending at small |cbar| by up to 1.1e-3 in the upper tail), the law of U at the edge of its support (4.4e-7), and the parts at the root of the parabola on the vertical line, which now go to Filon's rule (the linear form was off by up to 4e-3 above the unit root and by 1e-4 with GLS detrending at small |cbar|, the t-ratio with c0 < 0 by 8.5e-5 and its density by 1e-2). On the line ADFmax and parabolas with two roots (c0 > 0 with x < 0) keep the Gauss-Legendre rule and its warning.

Above the unit root the t-ratio with c0 != c has its upper tail at x of the order (c - c0) e^c/(2c). There the line integral's inner rule exceeds the bounds of the engine (from x ~ 700), and in case DK the classical engine takes the conditional integral with its checks whatever its flag (its one evaluation is off by up to 14 there, and before November 23, 2026 the default route returned it where the checked value was flagged: values outside [0, 1]); for c > 4 the three cases take the conditional integral at x > 300 too, where the line integral's cost grows like x^4 (in cases DU, DT at c = 16 it exhausted the memory at the median), and case DK with c0 > c at x < -20, where the line integral is off by up to 1.5e-3. Against an independent computation (the law of V given J(1), scratch/fut/bridge_ref.jl) the values of case DK are within 1.3e-8 up to c = 14, those of cases DU, DT within 5e-7 of the large-c law at c = 13 and 14; above c = 14 the classical engine refuses such points with an ArgumentError (the conditional integral is off by 7.7e-5 in case DK at c = 16 and gives 1e44 in case DT, and the general engine's line exceeds its bounds). The general engine cuts its line to the oscillation with c0 (it was off by 1.1e-3 at c = 10, c0 = 9). Every distribution function (ltratio_cdf, coef_cdf, dist_cdf, DFDet.coef_cdf) ends with a check of its values: below -1e-8 or above 1 + 1e-8 an error that names the call. The line integral's fixed node layouts serve -50 <= x <= 15, its adaptive rules the far tails beyond (where the layouts lost up to 2e-3), and no panel of their partitions ends within 1e-9 of the end of the partition (a panel a few ulps long put its nodes on the support edge of the kernel: NaN at the ulps above x = -500/4^k); the general engine's paths take any tiny x.
