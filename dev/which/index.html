Which engine

dev/engine_table.jl (dev/engine_table.txt) times both engines in each cell (4 threads, the warm time of one call, BenchmarkTools minimum of 2; F and f at x = mean + (-2, -1, 0, 1, 2) sd for the t-ratio) and compares their values; where they differ by more than 1e-8 it compares each with the other's checked computation (verified = true). The rule, autoengine in src/merged.jl, was set to take the faster engine at the same accuracy, and the right one where they disagree. The times in the table below are those of the run of November 26, 2026 (version 0.5.0, the thinner rules of one evaluation included, dev/engine_table.txt; an M4 laptop on battery power, about five minutes for the whole table): the general engine's paths take about half the time they took when the rule was set, so that in some cells the rule now takes the slower engine, which the last column says ("now"); the rule is unchanged in this release.

Within the general engine, dist_cdf, dist_pdf and dist_quantile (and the classical functions with a design, through them) choose between two representations by the keyword method: :auto (the default) takes the line integral with the explicit kernel where it covers the design and c0 = 0, and the inversion on paths around the roots otherwise; :line forces the line integral (an ArgumentError with the reason where it does not cover the design, the start or the options) and :paths the inversion on paths. The line integral covers the designs whose numerator has rank one or two: no deterministic terms, the continuous designs that do not vanish at the origin and whose span is closed under differentiation (polynomial trends of any order, an intercept with sines and cosines), and detrend_first of a continuous design that does not vanish at the origin, with a zero or a stationary start and least squares detrending (not GLS detrending, a fixed start or a design with a jump). It is the classical engine's integrator with P(t) and the two roots supplied by the general engine's transform (src/DFDet_line.jl). On warm calls at c0 = 0 (November 26, 2026, after the thinner rules of the paths; F and f at five points, four threads) it is 1.03 to 4.1 times faster than the paths below the unit root (median 1.8; 2.8 to 4.1 times without deterministic terms and a zero start, 1.05 to 1.3 times at c = -50 with deterministic terms; no deterministic terms, an intercept, the linear and the quadratic trend and the trend detrended first, zero and stationary starts, c = 0, -10, -50) and 1.06 to 1.23 times faster than the classical engine for the intercept and the trend; above the unit root, at c = 2, it is 1.3 to 3.8 times faster without deterministic terms and with an intercept but 1.6 to 1.8 times slower than the paths for the linear and the quadratic trend and the trend detrended first (the rule takes it there all the same); with c0 != 0 it is 4 to 10 times slower, hence the rule. A case string or a classical design with engine = :general at c0 = 0 therefore takes the general engine's line integral (as the general column of the table below at c0 = 0, except case DG, which the line integral does not cover); with method = :paths it takes the paths.

Where the classical engine computes, its values are those of 0.5.0 bit for bit; where the rule takes the general engine for a case string, the values move within the differences above. The borders were swept: the stationary start at c = -3e-3 (cases SU, ST: the general engine within 4e-11 of the classical at c = -3e-3, off by 8e-10 at c = -1e-3), the moments next to zero (case SK down to c = -3e-3, cases SU, ST down to c = -1), the power envelope (case DK within 3e-12 of the classical down to c = -0.01) and the tangency of case DK (the window 1e-3 above x*). The tests follow the rule (test/runtests.jl, test/engine): one stored value changed, the power envelope of case DK at c = -7, now 0.49940198281562 (it was 0.49940198260314117, the classical engine's bisection of the critical value to 1e-9; the same construction with the critical value bisected to 1e-14 is within 8e-13 of the new value), and the checks of the classical engine's own computations (ADFmax with a stationary start, the moments, quantiles and default values of its test grid far from the unit root, the power envelope) force engine = :classical, so that they keep their stored values. Three defects of the general engine are repaired in src/DFDet.jl (see NEWS): the law of V on the vertical line (off by 2e-3 at c = 5, and with GLS detrending at small |cbar| by up to 1.1e-3 in the upper tail), the law of U at the edge of its support (4.4e-7), and the parts at the root of the parabola on the vertical line, which now go to Filon's rule (the linear form was off by up to 4e-3 above the unit root and by 1e-4 with GLS detrending at small |cbar|, the t-ratio with c0 < 0 by 8.5e-5 and its density by 1e-2). On the line ADFmax and parabolas with two roots (c0 > 0 with x < 0) keep the Gauss-Legendre rule and its warning.

Above the unit root the t-ratio with c0 != c has its upper tail at x of the order (c - c0) e^c/(2c). There the line integral's inner rule exceeds the bounds of the engine (from x ~ 700), and in case DK the classical engine takes the conditional integral with its checks whatever its flag (its one evaluation is off by up to 14 there, and before November 23, 2026 the default route returned it where the checked value was flagged: values outside [0, 1]); for c > 4 the three cases take the conditional integral at x > 300 too, where the line integral's cost grows like x^4 (in cases DU, DT at c = 16 it exhausted the memory at the median), and case DK with c0 > c at x < -20, where the line integral is off by up to 1.5e-3. Against an independent computation (the law of V given J(1), scratch/fut/bridge_ref.jl) the values of case DK are within 1.3e-8 up to c = 14, those of cases DU, DT within 5e-7 of the large-c law at c = 13 and 14; above c = 14 the classical engine refuses such points with an ArgumentError (the conditional integral is off by 7.7e-5 in case DK at c = 16 and gives 1e44 in case DT, and the general engine's line exceeds its bounds). The general engine cuts its line to the oscillation with c0 (it was off by 1.1e-3 at c = 10, c0 = 9). Every distribution function (ltratio_cdf, coef_cdf, dist_cdf, DFDet.coef_cdf) ends with a check of its values: below -1e-8 or above 1 + 1e-8 an error that names the call.
